# Tenge Sense — Beyond the Monthly Payment

This is the PLS419 final-project website about household debt, consumer credit, рассрочка, mortgages, car loans, social pressure and FOMO in Kazakhstan.

## Current proposal

Tenge Sense is **not a standalone AI product**. It proposes strengthening Kazakhstan's existing public response—especially Qaryzsyz Qogam—through four connected actions:

1. Practical financial education in schools, colleges, universities and youth programmes.
2. FOMO and advertising literacy, using real Kazakhstan loan advertisements and social-media examples.
3. Clearer credit advertising and responsible-lending oversight, including attention to combined obligations and instalments.
4. Earlier access to independent debt advice and fair restructuring.

The website is a presentation-first public information and teaching tool: short story sections, large statistics, an advertising example gallery, a research summary, the national policy plan, a FOMO pause check and a local budget calculator. The calculator runs locally in the browser. AI is optional support for translation, plain-language explanations and sorting public advertisements for human review. It does not assess creditworthiness, recommend loans or access personal bank data.

## Evidence and limitations

Kazakhstan already has public financial-literacy and debt-support activity. The Government reported the Qaryzsyz Qogam project operating across regions, with legal support and financial education. The President's 2024 address called for financial-literacy and digital-hygiene education in schools and universities. The proposal strengthens and connects this work instead of creating a duplicate website or institution.

The four assessed sections contain approximately **3,576 words** based on the rendered text of the four section elements. Recount after edits and follow the syllabus's exact counting rule.

Do not combine indicators with different denominators. DEMOSCOPE is a self-reported adult survey, not a youth-only credit-register dataset. National Bank debt-service ratios, World Bank debt-to-wages ratios and BNS household expenditure shares measure different things. Sector profitability does not prove that every bank exploits borrowers or that all profits come from consumer credit. International research on debt and labour outcomes is not a Kazakhstan-specific causal estimate.

The ₸29–40 million programme budget is an illustrative 12-month planning range for curriculum, training, campaign content, advice, oversight and evaluation—not the cost of the website, not an approved government budget and not a supplier quote. The static website has near-zero marginal hosting cost if existing public infrastructure is used.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Create a public GitHub repository, for example `tenge-sense`.
2. Upload `index.html`, `styles.css`, `app.js` and `README.md` to the repository root.
3. Open **Settings → Pages**.
4. Choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then save.
5. Test the generated public URL on a phone and desktop.

The files are prepared but have not been published. A public URL exists only after deployment.
